Set up in minutes

  1. One minuteFirst, upload the zip under Plugins › Add New, like any plugin.
  2. Five minutesThen make a booking page: what you offer, who does it, your hours.
  3. SecondsNext, paste its shortcode onto any page of your site.
  4. All day, every dayAfter that it takes bookings while you work, and shows your day.

What people ask first

Does it work on WordPress.com's free plan?

No. WordPress.com's free plan does not let you add plugins. It runs on any WordPress you install yourself (most web hosts set it up for you), with WordPress 6.2 and PHP 7.4 or newer.

Where are the bookings kept?

In your own site's database, in tables the plugin makes. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and there is no account to make.

What if two people book the last free time at once?

One gets it, and the other is shown the times that are still free. Every booking is checked again under a lock in the database. We sent 10 bookings for the same time at the same moment: exactly one went through.

Does it take payments?

No. It books and confirms; payment stays at your counter. Where a VIP table has a minimum spend, the customer sees it and accepts it before booking.

Do customers need an account?

No. They book with a name and a phone number, get a booking code, and change or cancel through the link in their email.

What happens if I remove it?

Your booking pages and bookings stay in the database, so installing it again picks up where you left off. To remove everything, tick “Also delete every module, booking and customer it stored” under Booking › Settings first.
